## Title

DayFold

## Short description

Fold a day into a souvenir ticket.

## Full description

At the end of the day, DayFold prints one thing worth keeping. Pick a mood, write a line, add a place, a time, or a number. The app lays it out as a receipt, a train ticket, a boarding pass, or a postcard. Pull down to print, with a light haptic. Swipe the collection and fold a month into one summary. Share the image. No account.

Looks and DayFold Pro (more than 8 tickets) unlock with coins from printing a ticket, the daily reward, or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and DayFold Pro are one-time unlocks.
